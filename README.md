# InfraFlow — Infrastructure Migration Command Center

InfraFlow is an independent product and project management case study exploring how enterprise IT teams can coordinate infrastructure migrations through centralized planning, dependency tracking, risk management, and change control.

## Key Focus Areas
- Migration planning and dependency mapping
- Risk and issue management

- Project governance and change approvals
- Milestone tracking and stakeholder reporting

## Documentation
1. **Discovery & Product Requirements** — Problem definition, target users, proposed solution, and MVP scope.
2. **PMO & Governance Pack** — RAID log, RACI matrix, milestone planning, reporting, and change control.

## Skills Demonstrated
Project Management · Business Analysis · PMO Governance · Requirements Analysis · Risk Management

**Project Status:** Concept and product definition; workflows and features are proposed, not yet implemented.
